What's covered

The 10 red flags in the checklist

Each one maps to a real place small businesses lose money without realizing it.

01

Vendor contracts on auto-renew

Terms you agreed to two years ago are still active — at rates you'd never accept today.

02

Commission plans misaligned with margin

Paying on revenue when you should be paying on margin is subsidizing the wrong deals.

03

No benchmark for business travel

Without a framework, every trip is its own negotiation — and nobody wins.

04

Scope creep with no change-order process

Work that wasn't scoped gets done anyway. The cost comes out of margin.

05

SaaS subscriptions nobody uses

The average small business is paying for 3–5 tools that have been dormant for months.

06

No CSR strategy — but real CSR spend

Giving without a program means no brand equity, no documentation, no return.

07

Payment terms that favor vendors, not you

Net-30 outbound while paying net-15 inbound is a silent cash flow squeeze.

08

No written vetting process for contractors

Hiring on trust costs more in rework and scope disputes than any vetting process would.

09

Expense reimbursement without policy

Without clear policy, reimbursements expand to fill whatever space is available.

10

No quarterly financial review cadence

Problems caught in Q4 cost twice what they would have cost to catch in Q2.
